Who We Are

About BORA Foundation

Transforming lives through compassion, dignity, and sustainable community development

Making a Difference Every Day

Creating Lasting Change
in Communities

BORA Foundation is dedicated to empowering underserved communities through innovative programs in education, healthcare, and sustainable development. We believe every individual deserves access to opportunities that enable them to reach their full potential.

Founded by a team bridging Germany and Rwanda, we operate with full transparency, compassion, and a deep respect for the dignity of every person we serve.

Our Mission

Creating lasting change through education, healthcare, and sustainable development initiatives in vulnerable communities.

Our Vision

A world where every community has the resources and support to thrive and reach its full potential.

Our Approach

Collaborative partnerships and grassroots initiatives guided by transparency, accountability, and human dignity.

What We Do

Our Programs

Three core pillars creating meaningful, sustainable impact in the communities we serve

01

Education

Laying the foundation for vulnerable children's futures by investing in their education. We pay school fees, provide uniforms, and cover all school requirements — helping children return to school and chase their dreams.

  • School Fees Payment
  • School Uniforms & Supplies
  • Educational Support
  • Mentoring & Guidance
Support Education
Most Needed
02

Healthcare

Supporting vulnerable families by providing medical insurance (Mutuelle de Santé). At just €5 per person annually, we make healthcare accessible to families who cannot afford it — ensuring preventive care and treatment.

  • Medical Insurance (Mutuelle)
  • Full Family Coverage
  • Preventive Care Access
  • Emergency Treatment
Fund Healthcare
03

Self-Sufficiency

Empowering vulnerable people — widows, widowers, single mothers, and orphans — by providing sewing machines, starting capital, and income-generating resources so they can earn a sustainable living.

  • Sewing Machines
  • Starting Capital
  • Skills Training
  • Income Generation
